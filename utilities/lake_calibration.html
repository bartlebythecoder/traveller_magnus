Lake Calibration

A lake fills only while its catchment delivers more water than its own surface evaporates — catchment ≥ K × lakeArea, with K driven by hydrographics (LAKE_K0 and LAKE_K_DECADE in js/terrain_field.js). Counting every closed basin instead gives a dry world MORE lakes than a wet one, which is backwards.

The calibration target is Earth, which is hydrographics 7: lakes cover about 2% of land. The count must also fall monotonically as the world dries, reaching effectively zero by hydrographics 1. Re-run this page after changing either constant, the pit-fill, or the flow accumulation.
