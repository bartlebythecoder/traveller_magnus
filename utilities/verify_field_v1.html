Terrain Field v1 Baseline

World images are never stored — they are recomputed from the seed on every view. So any change to the terrain field silently redraws every world in every existing sector. This page renders 30 images at terrainFieldVersion 1 and compares them against hashes recorded on 2026-09-13. They must all match. If any differs, version 1 has been disturbed and every user's existing worlds will look different after upgrading.
